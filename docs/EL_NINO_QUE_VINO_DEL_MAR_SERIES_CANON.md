# El niño que vino del mar — Series Canon

Status: strategic canon, not a fixed production plan.

This document supersedes the older default assumption that the project should be organized as an original forty-chapter spine. The forty-spine can remain as historical inventory, but it is no longer the governing architecture.

## 0. Core editorial law

- Do not publish by chronology.
- Publish by pressure.
- Each chapter must stand on its own.
- Each volume must stand on its own.
- The full series works by refraction, not repetition.
- A story may return only if it changes function.
- The reader should feel life, not the filing system.

## 1. Series shape

The series is a mother-series made of independent volumes. Each volume is governed by a dominant current, informally called a colour.

The colours are not pure compartments. They are editorial gravity fields.

A chapter belongs to a colour because its center of gravity is there, not because every scene has been purified to that hue. Real life is not pure blue, green, yellow, or gray. It is bluish, greenish, yellowed by memory, or crossed by several currents at once.

The goal is not to sever stories artificially so they match a colour. The goal is to let each story breathe while keeping the volume's dominant pressure legible.

## 2. Invisible architecture

The architecture exists for the author and editor.

It must not be telegraphed to the reader.

The reader may discover coherence, but should not see machinery. If the structure starts announcing itself, reduce it. A volume should feel like a book, not an artificially severed limb designed to obtain the correct hue.

## 3. Currents, not compartments

Each volume has a dominant current and counter-currents.

Counter-currents are allowed and desirable. Josh grows, learns, misreads, listens, masks, repairs, fears, eats, remembers, and invents all at the same time. The books should preserve that simultaneity.

A suggested balance for the Yellow volume:

- 70% Yellow: childhood perception, mask, language, school, reading emotional weather.
- 10% Green: house, trees, food, kitchen, objects, tools, soil.
- 8% Blue: origin, sea, distance, names, departure, Montreal/San Lorenzo as future echo.
- 5% Gray: documents, places for things, archive, ledgers, paper as later danger.
- 4% Orange: food, body, hunger, sweetness, shame, domestic desire.
- 3% future echoes: small promises for later volumes.

These numbers are editorial guidance only. They should not appear in the work.

## 4. Narrative canon

### 4.1 Objects precede theory

Objects carry meaning before explanation does. If an object can communicate the idea, remove the lecture.

### 4.2 Promise before explanation

Future volumes may be seeded through promises: small callbacks or unresolved edges that work inside the current chapter even if the reader never reads another volume.

A promise must not feel like a trailer, lore dump, or note to the reader. It must function locally first.

### 4.3 Refracted recurrence

A recurring story is not a remake. It returns only when it has another function.

Examples:

- La guaparra in Yellow: tool, pressure, learning the difference between sharp and cruel.
- La guaparra in Gray: evidence, document, danger of cutting too much.
- La guaparra in White: return the blade to the nail.

### 4.4 Josh observes, but is not omniscient

Childhood interpretation may be wrong. That is not unreliability as gimmick. It is how children construct reality while inhabiting it.

A scene may later reveal that Josh misread the adult weather. This should strengthen the book, not make it a puzzle box.

### 4.5 Sideways humour

The books need quiet humour to avoid solemnity and pressure sickness.

Preferred humour:

- British understatement.
- Jewish self-interruption.
- literary mischief.
- anti-symbolic deflation.
- unnecessary precision.
- quiet absurdity.

Avoid sitcom timing and visible punchlines.

Examples of acceptable direction:

- The tree did not know it would someday have symbolic obligations.
- Black is too theatrical.
- A word does not need permission from the academy if it has cut enough rope.
- The coffee was tired.

### 4.6 Perceptual leaks

Sensory language may escape ordinary categories without explanation.

Examples:

- a smell can be green.
- spoons can sound round.
- the rain can enter with shoes.
- coffee can be tired.

Trust the reader.

### 4.7 Literature may mock literature

The narrator may occasionally deflate his own symbolism. This prevents objects from becoming dead emblems too early.

Use sparingly.

## 5. Projected colour currents

These are projections, not commitments.

- Yellow: childhood, masks, language, school, reading emotional weather.
- Green: house, trees, kitchen, soil, tools, domestic hunger.
- Blue: sea, origin, migration, names, rivers, Montreal, San Lorenzo.
- Red: Debate, press, threat, censorship, public war.
- Orange: hunger, body, kitchen, desire, shame.
- Violet: Aurora, Unicorn Dreams, suspended May, children who were not born.
- Gray: documents, legal memory, omissions, negative ledger, archive.
- White: Leuchtturm, orientation, lighthouse, agency, closing movement.
- Black: quarantine, material not yet public, private mycelium.

## 6. Active volume: Yellow

Working strategic identity:

Yellow is not simply a childhood book. It is the book where the observer is made.

The active function is to show how Josh learns to see, register, compare, repair, mask, misunderstand, name, and survive adult weather before he has adult language.

Yellow should feel complete as a book. If the reader never reads another volume, Yellow must still close as a finished work. If the reader continues, they should later realize they had been walking through the foundation without ever being shown the blueprint.

### 6.1 Yellow working title

Current provisional title:

La casa aprendió antes que el niño

### 6.2 Yellow active candidates

Current candidates include:

- What This Book Is Allowed to Be / El libro no es tribunal.
- The House Before the Name / La casa antes del nombre.
- La primera máscara.
- La guaparra de Abuela.
- Don Xavier y las monedas enormes.
- El niño que coleccionaba bisagras.
- Lenguas, sinagoga, memoria.
- El pestillo antes de la noche.
- Children as Witnesses of Weather.
- No nombres el árbol nuevo todavía.

This is not a final table of contents. It is the active field.

### 6.3 Yellow repair strategy

Do not repair Yellow by cutting it into pure colour.

Repair it by breathing:

- Add ordinary life.
- Add food, boredom, neighbours, dogs, insects, weather, coins, buses, school smells, radio, television, and small failures.
- Add counter-current chapters where needed.
- Reduce visible explanation.
- Let objects carry load.
- Seed future promises without announcing them.

## 7. Don Xavier seed

Title:

Don Xavier y las monedas enormes

Dominant current:

Yellow.

Secondary currents:

Green, Blue, Gray, Orange.

Function:

The child learns trust, place, savings, belonging, sweetness, and loss without anyone explaining them.

Objects:

- red truck.
- huge coins.
- glass coin jar.
- coffee in a glass.
- orange gummies.

Editorial rule:

Do not explain Don Xavier's disappearance. Do not convert him into a clean symbol. Let him leave in the red truck.

Possible closing movement:

The gummies wait too long. The coin jar remains Abuela's. The truck keeps leaving in memory. Some people teach trust once and disappear before checking the homework.

## 8. Seedbank entry format

Each new seed should be stored with:

- Object.
- Scene.
- Dominant current.
- Counter-currents.
- Function.
- Risk.
- Possible volume.
- Future echo.
- State: raw / developed / quarantine / ready.

## 9. Promises and callbacks

A promise is an allusion placed in the current volume to open narrative debt toward a future story without explaining it.

Examples:

- La guaparra returns as ethics of writing.
- May appears before Aurora.
- Debate appears as background noise before the Red volume.
- The archive appears as a domestic object before the Gray volume.
- The sea appears as memory before the Blue volume.
- A tree appears before it receives symbolic duties.

## 10. Caution for sibling GPT / external workers

Do not default back to the original forty-spine as the controlling plan.

Do not purify chapters by colour.

Do not make the machinery visible.

Do not turn this into chronology, court, archive dump, or doctrine.

Use the forty-spine only as inventory if needed. The current governing architecture is colour-current volume design with invisible structure, counter-currents, promises, and self-standing chapters.
