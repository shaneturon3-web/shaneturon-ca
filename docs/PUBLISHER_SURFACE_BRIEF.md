# Publisher Surface Brief

Publisher is the clean public landing page for Shane Turon's writing catalogue.

It is not an admin console, source gate, backstage page, taxonomy engine, or archive dump. It presents works as the public reader should encounter them: books, essays, stories, and archive items.

## Surface rule

The public category is a presentation decision, not an ontology.

A work can be called a guide, essay, tactical manual, training material, or instruction inside its title without forcing the site to create a matching category. The landing page keeps a clean public structure even when the work itself plays with genre, title, or form.

## Current public categories

- Books
- Essays
- Stories
- Archive

There is no public Manuals section. Manual-like works live under Books when they are presented as books.

There is no public Backstage section. If a surface contains internal/source/safety language, remove it from the clean website surface rather than protecting or hiding it.

## Landing behavior

The Publisher landing page uses one catalogue rail and one editorial stage.

The rail selects a category. The stage displays the selected category and its works. Selecting a work shows an editorial preview and read action when a route exists.

## Public copy rule

Do not expose internal catalogue language on the public surface.

Forbidden public labels include: Shelf, publicSafe, sourceStatus, routeEnabled, source gate, backstage, raw dump, source discipline, internal/restricted, and Drive/Gmail/Blogger as machinery explanations.

## Content rule

Do not ingest or dump source documents into Publisher. External source surveys can inform architecture and item discovery, but publication requires explicit selection and clean editorial handling.
